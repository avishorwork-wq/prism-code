Prism Code

A color code you can scan, lock and sign.

  • Data227 cells
  • Error correction96 cells
  • Header72 cells
  • Guide cells + border100 cells
  • Orientation pattern9 cells

A real 21 x 24 code, 504 cells, each one of 8 colors (3 bits). Cell positions, colors, counts and layer roles are the real encoder output. The damage and the beam paths are illustrative. The corner cells are filled with a fixed pattern, as the current encoder does.

Prism turns a message or a link into colored hexagons. Free, offline, and it reads on your own device.

Prism is like a QR code, but made of colored hexagons. Standard QR apps cannot read it, so people scan it with Prism.

Use QR when every phone has to read it. Prism's extras are the extra room in color and the Prism Secure lock and signature. Numbers are from desktop tests on synthetic images. Locked or signed codes hold less than the raw figure.

Lock it.
Sign it.

Prism Secure can lock a code with a password (AES-256-GCM) and optionally sign it (ECDSA P-256). It all happens on your device.

Try Prism Secure

Not independently audited. A weak password can be guessed from a photo. A signature shows the code is unchanged and made by one key, not who owns that key. No password recovery.
